# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Risk Owners

### Role Summary
Risk Owners identify, assess, and monitor project risks so that issues can be addressed before they disrupt delivery. They work closely with Project Managers and cross-functional partners to drive mitigation plans and escalate blockers when needed.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain a visible risk register for the project
- Assess probability, impact, and urgency of risks and issues
- Assign ownership for mitigation actions and follow-up
- Escalate significant risks to project leadership and stakeholders
- Track risk trends across the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Reduce uncertainty and disruption to delivery
- Ensure mitigation actions are assigned and completed
- Support proactive decision-making for project health

### Typical Communication
- Weekly risk reviews and project health check-ins
- Escalation updates to project managers and stakeholders
- Risk mitigation discussions with product and engineering leads

### Interacts with
- Project Managers to align risk prioritization and escalation paths
- Product Managers to understand business impact and trade-offs
- Developers to validate technical risks and mitigation feasibility

---

## Communications Leads

### Role Summary
Communications Leads manage how project updates are shared across stakeholders, teams, and leadership. They ensure that messages are consistent, timely, and aligned with the project plan.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain communication plans for internal and external audiences
- Coordinate stakeholder updates, briefings, and summaries
- Share decisions, action items, and changes in priority clearly
- Ensure critical project information reaches the right audiences
- Support executive reporting and team alignment

### Goals
- Improve transparency and stakeholder confidence
- Reduce confusion caused by inconsistent updates
- Keep teams informed without creating communication overload

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder updates and status summaries
- Steering committee briefings and project reporting
- Team announcements and decision logs

### Interacts with
- Project Managers to align messaging with project milestones and risks
- Product Managers to communicate roadmap status and trade-offs
- Developers to share delivery updates and blockers with context

---

## Quality Assurance Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, test strategy, and validation checkpoints for the project. They work with developers and stakeholders to confirm that the solution meets requirements before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality gates, acceptance checks, and test strategy
- Monitor validation coverage across features and releases
- Coordinate issue triage, defect prioritization, and regression reviews
- Partner with development teams on test readiness and release criteria
- Validate that outcomes meet agreed standards and user expectations

### Goals
- Improve release confidence and reduce defects escaping to production
- Maintain consistent quality standards across project phases
- Support a predictable, trusted delivery process

### Typical Communication
- Test readiness reviews and release sign-off checkpoints
- Defect triage and regression planning conversations
- Quality metrics shared with project and product leadership

### Interacts with
- Developers to align on validation criteria and defect ownership
- Project Managers to coordinate quality milestones and release readiness
- Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and user impact

---

## Resource Managers

### Role Summary
Resource Managers ensure that the right people, skills, and capacity are assigned to the project at the right time. They help teams plan workload and reduce bottlenecks, allowing delivery to proceed smoothly.

### Responsibilities
- Forecast team capacity and skill coverage
- Balance workload across project contributors and teams
- Identify staffing gaps, dependencies, and constraints
- Support hiring, allocation, or partner coordination decisions
- Track delivery capacity against project needs and schedule commitments

### Goals
- Improve capacity planning and reduce burnout
- Maintain sustainable team performance across delivery phases
- Support realistic commitments and timeline management

### Typical Communication
- Capacity planning discussions and staffing reviews
- Allocation updates with project leads and stakeholders
- Dependency management conversations across teams

### Interacts with
- Project Managers to align staffing with schedule and milestone plans
- Product Managers to understand prioritization and sequencing impacts
- Developers to identify workload constraints and support needs

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction, guardrails, and architectural clarity for the solution. They ensure the implementation is aligned to standards, scalability needs, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define solution architecture and technical standards
- Identify dependencies, integration patterns, and design constraints
- Review major technical decisions with engineering and leadership
- Guide trade-off analysis for scalability, reliability, and maintainability
- Support risk mitigation for platform and system-level concerns

### Goals
- Ensure the solution is technically sound and sustainable
- Reduce architectural drift and avoid costly rework
- Align delivery decisions with enterprise standards and business goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and technical decision logs
- Design discussions with engineering teams and stakeholders
- Planning conversations focused on trade-offs and constraints

### Interacts with
- Developers to guide implementation choices and technical quality
- Project Managers to align architecture decisions with project milestones
- Product Managers to translate business needs into feasible technical approaches

---

## Change Management Coordinators

### Role Summary
Change Management Coordinators help teams manage adjustments to scope, dependencies, and implementation plans without losing alignment across stakeholders. They ensure that changes are reviewed, communicated, and incorporated into delivery planning.

### Responsibilities
- Track change requests, impacts, and approval decisions
- Coordinate communication for schedule, scope, or priority changes
- Assess downstream impacts on risk, capacity, and delivery commitments
- Support readiness reviews for impacted teams and stakeholders
- Maintain documentation for decision history and outcomes

### Goals
- Reduce confusion from uncontrolled changes
- Improve consistency between project planning and execution
- Make sure changes are visible, understood, and approved before implementation

### Typical Communication
- Change review forums and approval discussions
- Status updates on scope and schedule adjustments
- Impact briefings for stakeholders and delivery teams

### Interacts with
- Project Managers to assess impacts on timelines and risk
- Product Managers to evaluate scope trade-offs and priority changes
- Developers and QA Leads to coordinate rollout readiness and validation needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Project work becomes clearer when each role has a defined responsibility, decision path, and relationship to other personas in the delivery process.
